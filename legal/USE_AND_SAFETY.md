and distribute the program. This document provides practical guidance and does

## Designs and quantities

Drawings, dimensions, sections, automatic rooms, material quantities and cost
estimates depend on input geometry, units, classifications, rate assumptions
and supported object types. BOQ output is an estimate derived from those data;
it is not a certified measurement, contract valuation or guaranteed budget.
An unsupported object or modeling assumption can change the result. Check
quantities against the drawings and the measurement rules for your project.

Have an appropriately qualified person review construction, structural,
fire/life-safety, accessibility, site, legal-boundary and regulatory decisions.
The software does not issue building permits, certify local code compliance,
or authorize professional practice. No standards-body or government
certification is represented by this release.

## Interchange and AI

Save backups before importing, converting or accepting generated edits.
DXF/IFC/STEP/PDF interchange may lose unsupported content. DWG support depends
on an independently installed converter. Review AI proposals and geometry;
generated text and actions can be inaccurate. An AI response is not a
professional opinion or warranty of originality or compliance.
