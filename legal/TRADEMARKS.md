# PY.cad name, logo and other marks

The publisher selected PY.cad as the display name on 19 October 2026 (India).
It replaces the visible PyCAD wordmark. The teal/navy geometric symbol and native
app icons remain; the raster wordmark was updated with the built-in image tool.
See assets/BRANDING.md. The artwork is AI generated; copyright eligibility and
exclusivity have not been established. The GPL grant covers licensable rights
actually held and does not manufacture rights that do not exist.

## Name review remains open

Capitalization and punctuation are not a finding of trademark distinctiveness.
PY.cad, PyCAD, PYCAD, PY CAD and similar forms need assessment for relevant goods,
services and territories. A public-use search identified an existing pycad
package, a pyCAD/OpenCAD library and PYCAD medical-imaging software. These are
public name-use findings, not a determination of registration ownership, priority,
enforceability or infringement. No other rights-holder was contacted.

The official Indian search portal exposed CAPTCHA/OTP login, and the newer search
route required login. WIPO's brand database exposed a verification challenge.
No authenticated registry result set or complete word/device clearance was
obtained. No application was submitted or fee paid. No registered status is
claimed. This display update is not a resolution of those questions.
