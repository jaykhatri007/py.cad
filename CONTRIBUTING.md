# Contributing to PY.cad

The maintainer is Jay Kundanlal Khatri. A public repository and submission channel
have not yet been established. Recipients may study, modify and redistribute
under the applicable licenses; this workflow does not limit those permissions.

Provide only material you have permission to offer under the applicable license.
Identify copied/adapted material, sources and licenses; retain original notices.
Do not submit employer/client secrets, proprietary drawings, credentials or
unintended personal data. Disclose material AI assistance and actual human work.

Original project material is offered under GPL-3.0-only where licensable rights
exist and are held. Upstream components retain their terms. Contributions need
license-compatibility and functional review; use synthetic examples and check
that changes preserve existing drawings and private data.

Submission does not itself assign copyright or give the maintainer exclusive
ownership. No CLA or DCO process has been adopted. If later selected, publish the
exact terms before requesting acceptance.
